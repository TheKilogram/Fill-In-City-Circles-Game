City Fill - Map Quiz

Goal: Fill the map by naming cities in the selected region. Each valid city drops a circle centered on that city. Cities meeting the population cutoff that fall inside any circle appear as small dots. Choose a difficulty to change circle radius.

  • Start typing a city name (e.g., "Denver" or "Paris") and press Enter.
  • Try to cover as much of the selected region as possible.
  • Reset clears everything to try again.
